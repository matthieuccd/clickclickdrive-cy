The Cyprus driving licence application form confuses a lot of people. The fields are in Greek, the instructions are brief, and one wrong entry can send you back to square one. This guide walks through every part of the form in plain English so you can fill it in right the first time.

## What is the Cyprus driving licence application form and where do you get it?

The application form is an official document issued by the Department of Road Transport, known in Greek as Τμήμα Οδικών Μεταφορών, or ΤΟΜ. You use it whether you are applying for a first licence or renewing an existing one.

You cannot download a valid form online and print it at home. You must collect a physical copy from a ΤΟΜ district office. Offices are located in Nicosia, Limassol, Larnaca, Paphos, and Famagusta (in the government-controlled area). Each office serves the residents of that district, so go to the one closest to where you live.

When you arrive, ask the reception desk for the driving licence application form. Staff will give you the correct version for your situation. There are different forms for first-time applicants and for renewals, so be specific about what you need.

The form is printed in Greek. Do not let that put you off. The layout follows a logical order: personal details first, then licence category, then declarations. Once you know what each section is asking, it is straightforward.

If you want the bigger picture before you visit ΤΟΜ, the [complete Cyprus driving licence guide](/en/blog/cyprus-driving-licence-complete-guide) explains the full process from start to finish. Reading it before your appointment will save you time.

Take a pen with you. Black ink is standard for official forms in Cyprus. Bring all your documents too, because staff may check them on the spot and point out anything that is missing.

## Which documents do you need before filling in the form?

Gathering your documents before you touch the form is the smartest move. Missing one item means a wasted trip.

Here is what you need:

- **Valid identity document** - a Cypriot identity card or a valid passport. EU citizens typically use their national ID card. Non-EU residents usually need their passport plus their residency permit.
- **Two recent passport-sized photographs** - these must be recent. Use a photo taken within the last six months. Standard passport photo rules apply: plain background, face forward, no sunglasses.
- **Proof of Cyprus address** - the form requires your address of ordinary residence in Cyprus. A utility bill, a rental contract, or a similar official document works in most cases. Check with ΤΟΜ what they accept.
- **Medical certificate** - required in certain cases (see the section below on medical certificates).
- **Any existing driving licences** - if you hold a foreign licence, bring the original. You will need the details from it for the declaration section of the form.

EU applicants and non-EU applicants both need to meet minimum residency requirements set by ΤΟΜ. These rules exist to confirm that Cyprus is your ordinary place of residence. If you are new to the island and unsure whether you qualify, the guide on [how to get a Cyprus driving licence as a foreigner](/en/blog/how-to-get-driving-licence-cyprus-foreigner) covers residency rules in detail.

Do not bring photocopies unless ΤΟΜ has specifically told you they are acceptable. Bring originals and let the staff make copies if needed.

## How do you complete the personal details section?

This is the first and longest section of the form. It asks for basic information about you. Take your time here. Small errors - a wrong date of birth, a misspelled name - are the most common reason forms get sent back.

Work through the fields in this order:

1. **Full name** - write your name exactly as it appears on your passport or identity card. If your ID uses all capital letters, write it in capitals on the form too.
2. **Date of birth** - use the day/month/year format. Cyprus follows the European convention.
3. **Place of birth** - this means the city or country where you were born. Use the English version if the form has a bilingual field; otherwise write clearly in block capitals.
4. **Nationality** - write your nationality as it appears on your passport.
5. **Identity document number** - copy this exactly from your ID card or passport. Double-check every digit.
6. **Cyprus address** - write your full residential address in Cyprus. This must be your actual address, not a P.O. box. The address should match your proof-of-residence document.
7. **Contact details** - a local phone number is usually required. Some versions of the form also ask for an email address.

If any field does not apply to you, draw a single horizontal line through it. Do not leave it blank, as blank fields can look like oversights to the processing officer.

Your name on the form must match your name on every supporting document. Even a small difference - an extra middle name or a hyphenated surname written differently - can cause problems. If your documents use different name formats, ask a ΤΟΜ officer before you submit.

## How do you declare your licence category and any existing licences?

This section tells ΤΟΜ what type of licence you are applying for. The standard category for driving a private car in Cyprus is Category B. If you are applying to drive a motorcycle, a truck, or a bus, different categories apply. The guide to [Cyprus driving licence categories](/en/blog/cyprus-driving-licence-categories) has a full breakdown of every category and what it covers.

Mark the category you are applying for clearly. If the form uses tick boxes, tick only the box that applies. Do not circle it or write next to it - tick it.

Below the category section, you will find a declaration about existing licences. This is where many applicants make mistakes. You must declare every driving licence you currently hold or have previously held. That includes:

- A licence from your home country
- A licence from another EU member state
- Any Cyprus learner permit you may hold

Write the licence number, the issuing country, and the expiry date for each one. If you are exchanging a foreign licence for a Cyprus one, the foreign licence details go here. Do not omit a licence because you think it has expired or is no longer valid. Disclose everything and let ΤΟΜ decide how it affects your application.

If you have never held any licence before, write "none" or draw a line through the field. Again, do not leave it blank.

## What do you need to know about the medical certificate and signature?

Not every applicant needs a medical certificate. For Category B (private car), a medical certificate from a registered doctor is required if you are over 65 years of age. ΤΟΜ sets the exact age threshold, so confirm the current rule directly with them, as it can change.

If you do need a medical certificate, get it from a doctor registered with the Cyprus Medical Council. The certificate must confirm that you are medically fit to drive. It should be recent - do not use one that is months old. ΤΟΜ may reject it if it looks out of date.

The certificate must be attached to your form when you submit. Do not fold it. Keep it flat inside a document wallet if you can.

At the bottom of the form, there is a declaration section. By signing it, you confirm that everything you have written is true and accurate. Read this section carefully before you sign. It is a legal declaration. Providing false information is a serious matter.

Sign your name in the space provided. Use the same signature you use on official documents. If the form asks for a date alongside your signature, write the date of the day you are submitting - not the day you filled it in.

## What are the most common mistakes and how do you avoid them?

ΤΟΜ staff see the same errors again and again. Knowing what they are will help you avoid them.

**Name does not match identity documents.** This is the top reason for rejection. Check every document you are bringing. Your name on the form must be identical to your name on your ID or passport.

**Wrong date format.** Some applicants write month/day/year because that is the convention in their home country. In Cyprus, use day/month/year.

**Missing photographs.** You need two photos. Bring two. Some applicants bring one and assume it is enough.

**Blank fields.** Every field needs an entry. If a field does not apply, draw a line through it. A blank field looks like a mistake.

**Not declaring a foreign licence.** Even if your foreign licence has expired, declare it. ΤΟΜ needs the full picture of your driving history.

**Old or missing medical certificate.** If you need one, it must be current. Check the date before you attach it.

**Using the wrong form.** First-time applicants and renewal applicants use different forms. Confirm at the desk which one you need.

**Writing in pencil.** Use black or blue pen. Pencil is not accepted on official forms.

## What happens after you submit your application to the Department of Road Transport?

Once you hand in your completed form and documents, a ΤΟΜ officer checks them. If everything is in order, your application is accepted. If something is missing or incorrect, you will be told on the spot and asked to correct it or return with the right documents.

After acceptance, you pay the applicable fee. Fees are set by the Republic of Cyprus. Check current amounts directly with ΤΟΜ, as they can change. Keep your receipt. It is proof that your application is being processed.

Processing times vary. For some applicants, the licence card is issued within a few days. For others, it can take longer, especially if checks need to be made against foreign licence records. ΤΟΜ will tell you the expected timeframe when you submit.

When your licence is ready, you collect it from the same district office, or it may be posted to your registered address depending on the current procedure. The finished product is a credit-card-sized document that meets EU standards. It is printed in both Greek and English, which makes it valid across EU member states.

If you submitted a foreign licence as part of an exchange, ΤΟΜ will retain it. You do not get it back. Make sure you are ready for that before you hand it over.

If your application is refused for any reason, ΤΟΜ will explain why in writing. You have the right to appeal. Ask the officer at the desk what the process is if that happens.

Getting the form right is genuinely half the battle. The process at ΤΟΜ is more straightforward than it looks on paper, especially once you know what each field is asking. Visit your nearest district office with a full document pack, take your time with every field, and do not hesitate to ask the staff for help. They deal with expat applicants regularly and are used to explaining the form in plain terms. A careful first submission is always faster than a corrected second one.

## FAQ

**Can I fill in the Cyprus driving licence application form in English?**
The form is printed in Greek, but you write your personal details - such as your name and address - in the Latin alphabet where the fields allow for it. If you are unsure how to handle a specific field, ask a ΤΟΜ officer at the district office for guidance before you write anything.

**Do I need to book an appointment at a ΤΟΜ district office to submit my form?**
Practice varies by district and can change over time. Some offices operate on a walk-in basis; others may require an appointment for certain services. Call your nearest ΤΟΜ office or check the official government portal before you travel to confirm the current system.

**What photographs are accepted with the Cyprus driving licence application form?**
You need two recent passport-sized photographs. They should show a clear, full-face view against a plain background. Photos must be recent - taken within the last six months is a safe rule. The same standards used for passport photos generally apply, so avoid sunglasses, hats, or heavy shadows.

**What happens if I hold licences from more than one country?**
You must declare all of them in the existing licences section of the form. Write the licence number, the issuing country, and the expiry date for each one. ΤΟΜ will assess them as part of your application. Omitting any licence, even an expired one, can cause your application to be rejected.

**How long does it take to get the Cyprus driving licence card after submitting the form?**
Processing times vary depending on the type of application and whether any checks need to be made against records in other countries. ΤΟΜ will give you an estimated timeframe when you submit your form. Keep your payment receipt as proof that your application is active while you wait.
