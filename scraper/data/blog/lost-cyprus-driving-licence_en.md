Losing your driving licence is stressful. You might not know where to start or how long the process will take. The good news is that Cyprus has a clear procedure. Follow the right steps and you will have a replacement in your hands faster than you think.

## What Should You Do Immediately After Losing Your Licence?

The moment you realise your licence is gone, stop and think about where you last had it. Check bags, jacket pockets, your car, and anywhere you recently paid for something. Sometimes it turns up quickly. If you have searched and cannot find it, accept that it is gone and move to the next step.

Do not wait. The sooner you report the loss and apply for a replacement, the better. A Cyprus driving licence is a credit-card-format document made to EU specifications. It contains a microchip. That means it holds personal data, and you want to cancel it before anyone else can misuse it.

If the licence was stolen rather than simply lost, act even faster. Theft opens the door to identity misuse. Getting a police report on record straight away gives you legal protection. It shows the authorities that you did not abandon the document or hand it over willingly.

While you sort things out, do not drive. Driving without a licence in Cyprus is a criminal offence. There is no grace period just because you lost it. The replacement process is your priority right now.

It also helps to take a few minutes to read up on how the system works. The [complete Cyprus driving licence guide](/en/blog/cyprus-driving-licence-complete-guide) covers the full picture, from categories to renewals, and gives useful background before you visit any office.

## How to Report the Loss to the Cyprus Police?

![Close-up of a Cyprus driving licence showing the EU credit-card format and microchip detail](/blog/lost-cyprus-driving-licence/inline-1.jpg)
*A Cyprus driving licence is a chipped, EU-format card - report its loss quickly to protect your data, via Pexels.com*

You must report the loss to the local police before you can apply for a replacement. This is not optional. The Department of Road Transport (Τμήμα Οδικών Μεταφορών, ΤΟΜ) will ask for a police report reference number as part of your replacement application.

Go to the nearest police station to where you lost the licence. If you are not sure which station covers your area, any station will be able to help or direct you. Bring your passport or national ID card so they can confirm who you are.

Tell the officer exactly what happened. Be honest about whether it was lost or stolen. The two are treated slightly differently. If it was stolen, the police will take the report more formally and this creates a record that protects you if the document is later used fraudulently.

The officer will give you a reference number for your report. Write it down and keep it safe. You will need this number at the ΤΟΜ office. Without it, your application cannot go forward.

The process at the police station is usually straightforward and does not take long. Most people are in and out within 30 minutes. Staff deal with lost document reports regularly, so they know the drill.

Once you have the reference number, you are ready to gather the rest of your documents and head to a ΤΟΜ district office. The police report and the reference number are the first building blocks of your replacement application.

## What Documents Do You Need for a Replacement?

Getting your paperwork right before you visit ΤΟΜ saves you a wasted trip. The office can be busy, especially in larger cities, so arriving with everything ready is smart.

Here is what you need to bring:

- **Police report reference number** - from the report you filed about the lost or stolen licence.
- **Valid identity document** - your national ID card or passport. Both are accepted. Make sure it is in date.
- **Completed application form** - available from any ΤΟΜ district office or downloadable from the official ΤΟΜ website. Fill it in before you go if you can.
- **Recent passport-sized photograph** - it must be recent. Check the photo requirements on the ΤΟΜ website or ask when you call ahead.
- **Payment for the replacement fee** - the amount is set by the Republic of Cyprus government. Check current fees with the Department of Road Transport before your visit.

That is the core list. If your circumstances are unusual - for example, if your address has changed since your last licence was issued - bring proof of your current address too. It is better to have too much than too little.

If you are unsure what [Cyprus driving licence categories](/en/blog/cyprus-driving-licence-categories) you hold and want to confirm before your visit, check your old licence if you have a photo of it, or ask ΤΟΜ when you call to confirm your appointment.

Double-check the application form at home. Errors on forms slow things down. Make sure your name is spelled exactly as it appears on your ID.

## How to Submit Your Application at the Department of Road Transport (ΤΟΜ)?

![Person standing at a government office counter handing documents to a staff member, driving licence replacement application](/blog/lost-cyprus-driving-licence/inline-2.jpg)
*Submitting your replacement application at a ΤΟΜ district office is a straightforward in-person process, via Pexels.com*

ΤΟΜ has district offices in Nicosia, Limassol, Larnaca, Paphos, and Famagusta (in the government-controlled area). Go to the office that covers the district where you live. If you live close to a district boundary, either office will serve you.

It is worth calling ahead before you go. Office hours can vary and some services require an appointment. A quick phone call saves you from making a wasted journey.

When you arrive, take a number and wait to be called. The staff at ΤΟΜ deal with licence replacements regularly. Hand over your documents in the order they ask. They will check your ID, review the application form, and verify your police report reference number.

If everything is in order, they will process your application and take your payment. They may also take a new photo at the counter or use the one you brought, depending on current procedures. Ask in advance so you know what to expect.

The replacement licence is a freshly produced document, not a duplicate of the old one. It will carry a new issue date. The validity period will generally reflect standard terms for your licence category - for EU/EEA citizens holding a category B licence, that is typically 10 years.

## How Much Does It Cost and How Long Does Replacement Take?

This is the question everyone asks first, and the honest answer is: it depends on when you are reading this. Replacement fees for a lost Cyprus driving licence are set by the Republic of Cyprus government and can change. Check current fees with the Department of Road Transport before your visit. Do not rely on figures you find on forums or social media groups - they are often out of date.

What is clear is that there is a fee. You will not get a replacement for free. Payment is made at the ΤΟΜ office when you submit your application.

Processing times can also vary. ΤΟΜ produces the replacement licence centrally, and it does not happen on the spot. Staff at the district office can give you a realistic estimate on the day you apply. In general, you should not expect it within 24 hours, but you also should not expect to wait months.

Some people ask whether they can speed up the process. The direct answer is to ask ΤΟΜ. They are the only ones who can tell you if any faster option exists.

## What If Your Lost Licence Was a Foreign or EU Licence?

The situation gets more layered if the licence you lost was not a Cyprus driving licence but a foreign or EU one. This happens more often than you might think. People move to Cyprus, keep driving on their home country licence, and then lose it.

If you hold an EU driving licence from another member state and you are a resident in Cyprus, the right move may be to exchange it rather than replace it through your home country. Read about [exchanging an EU driving licence in Cyprus](/en/blog/exchange-eu-licence-cyprus) to understand how that process works and whether it applies to your situation.

If you are a UK licence holder, the rules changed after Brexit. Cyprus and the UK have a bilateral agreement, but the details matter. Check the specifics through our guide on the [UK driving licence in Cyprus after Brexit](/en/blog/uk-licence-cyprus-after-brexit) before you take any action.

Non-EU residents who lost a foreign licence face a different path. You should contact both ΤΟΜ and your country's embassy or consulate in Cyprus. The embassy can help you get a replacement or certified copy from your home country. ΤΟΜ can advise on whether that document will be accepted in Cyprus or whether you need to go through a different process.

Do not assume that the same steps apply to every licence type. The rules differ depending on your nationality, your residency status, and the category of licence you hold. Getting the right advice first saves you from multiple trips and delays.

## How to Avoid Problems While Waiting for Your New Licence?

The gap between losing your licence and receiving the replacement is the riskiest period. You need to manage it carefully.

First and most important: do not drive. Cyprus law treats driving without a licence as a criminal offence. The fact that your licence is being replaced does not give you the right to drive in the meantime. This is not a grey area.

Carry your application receipt with you. If you are stopped by police during this period, the receipt shows that you have reported the loss and applied for a replacement. It is not a substitute for a licence, but it demonstrates that you acted responsibly.

If you need to get around, use taxis, buses, or ask a friend or family member to drive you. It is an inconvenience, but it is far less serious than a criminal charge for driving without a licence.

Tell your insurance company about the loss. Some policies require you to notify them when your licence is lost or stolen. Check your policy documents or call your insurer directly.

Keep the receipt and police report reference number stored digitally as well as on paper. A photo on your phone works well. If the paper copy is lost or damaged, you have a backup.

Finally, do not use platforms like [ClickClickDrive Cyprus](/en) to book a rental car during this period. Rental companies require a valid licence at the time of pickup. Your application receipt will not satisfy that requirement.

Once your replacement arrives, check all the details on it carefully. Make sure the name, date of birth, address, and licence categories are all correct. If anything is wrong, flag it with ΤΟΜ immediately.

Losing a driving licence is never convenient. But Cyprus has a clear, workable process for replacing one. Report the loss to police, gather your documents, visit a ΤΟΜ district office, and wait for your new card. Most people find the process straightforward once they know what is expected. The key is not to delay and not to drive until the replacement is in your hands.

## FAQ

**Do I need a police report to replace a lost Cyprus driving licence?**
Yes, a police report is required. You must visit a local police station, report the loss or theft, and get a reference number. ΤΟΜ will not process your replacement application without this reference number.

**Can I drive while I wait for my replacement Cyprus driving licence?**
No. Driving without a licence is a criminal offence in Cyprus. Carry your application receipt with you during this period, but do not get behind the wheel until the replacement document is in your hands.

**Where can I pick up the application form for a replacement licence?**
The application form is available at any ΤΟΜ district office in Nicosia, Limassol, Larnaca, Paphos, or Famagusta. You can also download it from the official ΤΟΜ website and fill it in before your visit.

**What happens if my Cyprus driving licence was stolen and someone tries to use it?**
The police report you file creates a legal record that the document was taken without your consent. This protects you if the licence is later used fraudulently. Report theft as soon as possible and keep your police reference number safe.

**I lost a foreign driving licence while visiting Cyprus - what should I do?**
Contact ΤΟΜ for guidance on your specific situation. You should also get in touch with your country's embassy or consulate in Cyprus, as they can help you obtain a replacement or certified copy from your home country. The process varies depending on your nationality and residency status.
