A licence issued in Northern Cyprus has no international recognition outside Turkey, as Northern Cyprus is not a recognised state under international law. If you hold a North Cyprus licence and want to drive legally in the Republic or abroad, you will need to obtain a Republic of Cyprus licence through the Department of Road Transport.

Two driving licences. One island. Very different legal realities. Whether you live in the north, the south, or somewhere else entirely and plan to visit Cyprus, understanding which licence actually works - and where - could save you a serious headache at a checkpoint or a car hire desk.

## What is the legal difference between the two licences?

The core difference is about statehood. The Republic of Cyprus is a recognised country. It has been an EU member state since 2004. Its laws, institutions, and documents carry weight in courts and governments around the world.

Northern Cyprus is different. It declared independence in 1983, but only Turkey recognises it as a sovereign state. No other UN member does. That matters enormously when it comes to driving licences, because a licence is only as valid as the authority that issues it.

The Republic of Cyprus Department of Road Transport, known by its Greek initials ΤΟΜ, is the only authority on the island that can issue a driving licence with international legal standing. Its licences follow the EU credit-card format set out in Directive 2006/126/EC. That format is a shared standard across all 27 EU member states.

A licence issued by authorities in Northern Cyprus is, from a legal standpoint, a document produced by an unrecognised administration. Other countries have no treaty obligation to accept it. Most do not.

This is not a political opinion. It is a practical legal fact that affects real people every day - students moving south for university, workers relocating, retirees who spent years in the north and now want to drive in Europe.

If you hold a North Cyprus licence and move to the Republic, you cannot simply swap it the way an EU citizen from Greece or Germany might exchange theirs. The north licence does not sit within any recognised exchange framework. You will need to go through the standard process based on your actual nationality.

## Where is a Republic of Cyprus licence recognised?

A Republic of Cyprus driving licence opens a lot of doors. Because Cyprus is an EU member, its licences are accepted in all 27 EU member states without any extra paperwork. You can drive in France, Germany, Spain, or anywhere else in the bloc on a Cyprus licence.

The UK is also on the list. After Brexit, many EU licences lost their automatic exchange status in Britain. Cyprus was one of the countries grandfathered into the UK's exchange agreements. So if you hold a Republic of Cyprus licence and move to the UK, you are in a stronger position than holders of some other EU licences. You can read more about this in our guide on [UK driving licence in Cyprus after Brexit](/en/blog/uk-licence-cyprus-after-brexit).

Beyond Europe, the Republic of Cyprus has bilateral arrangements with a number of other countries. The exact list changes over time, so always check with ΤΟΜ before you travel or relocate.

For everyday purposes, the Republic licence is also accepted at car hire desks across Europe and in many destinations worldwide. That is something a North Cyprus licence simply cannot offer.

The licence itself looks like any other EU photocard. It has your photo, personal details, and category codes on a standard-sized card. It is renewed periodically depending on your age and the vehicle category you are licensed for.

## Why is a North Cyprus licence not internationally recognised?

The answer comes down to one word: recognition. International law works on the basis that states recognise each other. When a government issues a driving licence, other governments accept it because they accept the issuing state as legitimate.

Northern Cyprus has not cleared that bar. Turkey is the only UN member that recognises it. Every other country, including all EU member states and the UK, does not. That means there is no legal framework for other countries to accept documents issued by Northern Cyprus authorities.

The EU, in particular, has a clear position. EU driving licences are governed by shared directives. Only licences issued by EU member states fall within that system. Northern Cyprus is not an EU member state. Its licences are therefore outside the system entirely.

This affects more than just driving. It is the same reason a Northern Cyprus passport is not accepted for travel outside Turkey, and why Northern Cyprus property documents are not recognised in Republic of Cyprus courts. It is a consistent legal reality across all document types.

For drivers, the practical result is this: if you drive in the Republic of Cyprus, in any EU country, or in the UK on a North Cyprus licence alone, you may be doing so without a legally valid driving document. That creates real risk - for insurance, for liability, and for legal standing if you are involved in an accident.

## What happens when you cross the dividing line by car?

![Car approaching a road checkpoint in Cyprus, crossing point between north and south](/blog/north-cyprus-vs-republic-driving-licence/inline-1.jpg)
*Vehicles cross between the two sides of Cyprus through official checkpoints, where different rules apply on each side, Photo by Ishara Kasthuriarachchi via Pexels.com*

Crossing through one of the official checkpoints is straightforward for most people on foot or by car. But there are some things to know before you drive across.

If you are driving a vehicle registered in the Republic of Cyprus into the north, the Republic permits this through official checkpoints. However, your standard car insurance may not cover you once you cross. This is important. Contact your insurer before you go and ask specifically whether your policy covers driving in the north. Some policies do. Many do not. You may need to buy additional cover at the checkpoint.

Now, the licence question. If you hold a Republic of Cyprus licence and drive north, that licence is accepted as a valid driving document in the north. The north recognises it in practice, even if the political relationship between the two sides is complicated.

Going the other way is where it gets harder. If you hold only a North Cyprus licence and drive a vehicle into the Republic, you are technically driving without a recognised licence in the eyes of Republic law. The Republic does not recognise the north's administrative authority, and that includes its driving licences.

If you are a foreign national who learned to drive in the north and holds a licence issued there, the Republic will look at your underlying nationality when you apply for a licence in the south - not at your north Cyprus licence.

The rules at the checkpoints themselves focus mainly on vehicle documents and insurance. But if you are stopped by Republic police while driving on the southern side, your north Cyprus licence will not be treated as a valid document.

## How can someone with a North Cyprus licence get a Republic licence?

This is the most common practical question, and the answer depends on your nationality.

The key point is that ΤΟΜ does not treat a North Cyprus licence as an exchangeable document. It is not part of any bilateral agreement. So the north Cyprus licence itself does not get you a shortcut.

What matters instead is where you are actually from.

If you are an EU citizen, you can exchange your home-country EU licence for a Republic of Cyprus licence without sitting tests again, subject to ΤΟΜ's standard conditions. So if you are, say, Greek or Romanian and you have been living in the north, you would exchange your Greek or Romanian licence - not your north Cyprus one. Our guide on [exchanging an EU driving licence in Cyprus](/en/blog/exchange-eu-licence-cyprus) walks through that process in detail.

If you are a non-EU national from a country that has a bilateral exchange agreement with Cyprus, a similar process may apply. Check with ΤΟΜ for the current list.

If you are a non-EU national from a country with no agreement, you will generally need to sit both the theory test and the practical driving test in the Republic. The north Cyprus licence does not count as prior recognition. You are essentially starting the process as a new applicant. ## What driving licence categories does the Republic of Cyprus issue?

The Republic of Cyprus uses the same category system as the rest of the EU. If you have driven in any EU country, the codes will look familiar.

Category B covers standard passenger cars. It is the most common licence and the one most people apply for first. Other categories cover motorcycles, trucks, buses, and vehicles with trailers.

Here is a quick overview of the main categories:

- **Category AM** - mopeds and light quadricycles
- **Category A1, A2, A** - motorcycles of increasing engine size and power
- **Category B** - cars and light vehicles up to 3,500 kg
- **Category BE** - category B vehicle with a trailer
- **Category C1, C** - medium and large goods vehicles
- **Category D1, D** - minibuses and full-size buses

Licences are renewed periodically. The renewal schedule depends on your age and the category you hold. Older drivers and those licensed for large vehicles or passenger transport face more frequent renewals and may need medical checks.

The photocard format means all this information sits on a single credit-card-sized document. You can find a full breakdown in our article on [Cyprus driving licence categories](/en/blog/cyprus-driving-licence-categories).

If you are upgrading from category B to a higher category, you will need to meet additional requirements set by ΤΟΜ.

## Where do you go to start the process in the Republic?

All driving licence matters in the Republic of Cyprus go through the Department of Road Transport, ΤΟΜ. There is no other official route.

ΤΟΜ has district offices across the Republic. If you are based in or around Nicosia, that is where most people start. A driving school can help you prepare for the theory and practical tests if you need to sit them. You can find options through our list of [driving schools in Nicosia](/en/driving-schools/nicosia).

The process starts with submitting the right documents. What you need depends on your situation - your nationality, your current licence, and your residency status. Here is what most applicants will need to gather before they visit:

1. Valid passport or national ID
2. Proof of residence in the Republic of Cyprus
3. Your existing driving licence (home country licence, not a north Cyprus one)
4. Passport-size photos
5. Any medical certificates required for your age or licence category
6. Completed application form from ΤΟΜ

Fees apply at various stages. ΤΟΜ sets these and they can change. Do not rely on figures quoted online - check current fees with the Department of Road Transport directly.

If you need to sit tests, you will register with a licensed driving school. They handle the theory test booking and practical lesson records. Our [complete Cyprus driving licence guide](/en/blog/cyprus-driving-licence-complete-guide) covers the full process from start to finish, including what to expect on test day.

The waiting time for appointments varies. Book early, especially if you are working to a visa or residency deadline.

Wherever you are in your journey - newly arrived, relocating from the north, or simply updating your licence - the Department of Road Transport is the place to start. Their staff deal with these questions regularly and can tell you exactly what applies to your situation.

The gap between a North Cyprus licence and a Republic of Cyprus one is not just paperwork. It reflects a legal divide that has real consequences for anyone who wants to drive freely across Cyprus or travel in Europe. Getting the right licence sorted early saves a lot of trouble later.

## FAQ

**Is a North Cyprus driving licence valid in the Republic of Cyprus?**
No. The Republic of Cyprus does not recognise the administrative authority of Northern Cyprus, and that includes its driving licences. If you are stopped by police in the south while holding only a north Cyprus licence, it will not be accepted as a valid driving document.

**Can I exchange my North Cyprus licence for a Republic of Cyprus licence?**
Not directly. ΤΟΜ does not recognise a north Cyprus licence as an exchangeable document. The process you follow depends on your nationality - EU citizens exchange their home-country EU licence, while non-EU nationals may need to sit theory and practical tests in the Republic.

**Is a Republic of Cyprus driving licence valid in Northern Cyprus?**
In practice, yes. When you drive north through an official checkpoint, a Republic of Cyprus licence is accepted as a valid driving document. The north recognises it even though the political relationship between the two sides remains unresolved.

**Do I need special insurance to drive my Republic of Cyprus car into the north?**
Your standard car insurance may not cover you once you cross into the north. You should contact your insurer before crossing and ask specifically about coverage there. Some policies include it; many do not, and additional cover may be available at the checkpoint.

**Where do I apply for a driving licence in the Republic of Cyprus?**
All applications go through the Department of Road Transport, ΤΟΜ, which has offices in each district. Fees and document requirements change periodically, so check current information with ΤΟΜ directly before you apply.
